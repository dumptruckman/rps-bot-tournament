# Allow non-qualifying Challenger Teams

Status: accepted

An organizer may assign each Team the immutable role `competitor` or
`challenger` before sealing a Tournament. Competitor Teams are eligible for
playoff selection unless disqualified. Challenger Teams participate in the
same complete qualifying round robin, and their Series contribute normally to
their own and their opponents' qualifying standings and tie-break statistics,
but Challenger Teams are excluded when the playoff field is selected. A
Challenger Team therefore cannot become Tournament Champion.

This role is distinct from Disqualification. Challenger Fixtures are executed
normally and never create Administrative Series Wins merely because of the
role. If a Challenger Team commits a confirmed Security Violation, the ordinary
Disqualification rules still apply.

The Team Role is recorded in new Tournament plans, sealed Tournament Manifests,
and Scoreboard Projections. Omitted roles in legacy Team source mappings,
Tournament plans, and sealed Tournament Manifests mean `competitor`, preserving
their competitive behavior and resume compatibility.
